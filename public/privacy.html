Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Welcome to Math Matrix ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding any personal or device data collected during your gameplay sessions. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding this data.

1. Information We Collect

• Account Profiles: When syncing your progress via Google Sign-In or Email Sync, we securely store your email address, primary profile nickname, and a unique user identifier (UID).

• Gameplay Metrics: We save high scores, map stage progress, level choices, and streak lengths in order to maintain correct records and show accurate global rankings.

• Anonymous Performance Data: We may collect generic telemetry (crash logs, device model details) to optimize game layouts and prevent crashes across various Android devices.

2. How Your Data is Used

• Account Syncing: To backup your high scores and game progress across multiple devices seamlessly.

• Global Leaderboards: To show high scores on our global "Math Matrix Rankings".

• Game Optimization: To analyze general gameplay usage patterns and fix software bugs or latency issues.

3. Third-Party Integrations

• Firebase Services: We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore Database (hosted securely by Google Cloud) to manage accounts and database syncing.

• Google AdMob: We integrate Google AdMob to deliver standard in-game advertisements. AdMob may process non-sensitive device identifiers to personalize ads.

4. Your Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your game profile, high scores, and sync credentials at any time. To request deletion, please contact our support desk directly at:
